# Cluster Sales Manager

Cluster Sales Manager (cluster level, grade BP-IV). Accountable for revenue vs target of one cluster (1-3 kabupaten/kota) and leads the cluster sales team.
Key responsibilities:
- Own cluster revenue, net adds and active outlets
- Lead Sales Supervisors and Channel Officers; coach new joiners
- Coordinate the partner sales force (canvassers) through the distributor
- Propose and execute weekly action plans; escalate blockers to the Branch Manager
KPI ownership (A=accountable, R=responsible): REV_TOTAL (A), REV_DATA (A), REV_HOME_BB (R), SUBS_ACTIVE (A), NET_ADD (A), CHURN_RATE (A), OUTLET_ACTIVE (A).
