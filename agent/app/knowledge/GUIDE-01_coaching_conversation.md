# GUIDE-01 Coaching conversation for sales managers

*PT Nusantara Seluler (fictional). Owner: Head of People Partner (Sales).*

Use this guide when a team member's territory is below expectation on **controllable** drivers.

1. **Start with context, not the number.** Share the adjusted view: what part of the gap was outside their control (outages, competitor promos)?
2. **Look at drivers together.** Active outlets, effective-call rate, stock availability, net adds. Ask which one they believe is the biggest lever.
3. **Agree on 1-3 actions for the next two weeks**, each with a measurable check (e.g. "re-activate 12 priority outlets in Kecamatan X").
4. **Offer support.** Pairing with an experienced colleague, a sharing session with a peer cluster that is above expectation, or a backfill request if the team is short-staffed.
5. **Recognise effort and progress** at the next check-in, even when the target is still not met.

New joiners (tenure < 12 months) benefit most from structured coaching; prioritise them.
