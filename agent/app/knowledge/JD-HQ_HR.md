# Head of People Partner (Sales)

Head of People Partner (Sales) (national level, grade BP-II). Owns people processes for the sales organisation: performance cycle, staffing and coaching.
Key responsibilities:
- Run the performance management cycle in line with the PKB
- Approve staffing plans and backfill priorities
- Govern the use of people analytics and AI in HR decisions
KPI ownership (A=accountable, R=responsible): none.
