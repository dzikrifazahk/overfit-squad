# SOP-03 Handling revenue impact of network outages

*PT Nusantara Seluler (fictional). Owner: Regional Network Operations Manager. Version 2026.1.*

## Purpose
Separate network-caused revenue loss from sales performance so that sales teams are not held accountable for outages they cannot control.

## Steps
1. Network Operations logs every outage longer than 4 hours affecting 5 or more sites in the shock event register, with the affected desa.
2. Revenue Assurance estimates the prepaid revenue lost (baseline daily revenue x outage days x share of traffic lost).
3. The estimated loss is added back when computing **adjusted attainment** for the affected territories and people.
4. The Regional Network Operations Manager is the accountable owner of the recovery; the Cluster Sales Manager supports with customer retention offers after restoration.
5. Repeat outages (3+ in a quarter in the same kecamatan) are escalated to the Regional General Manager for a capex review.

## Service levels
- Site restoration: 4 hours (urban), 12 hours (rural), 48 hours (3T areas).
- Communication to affected outlets within 24 hours.
