# SOP-01 Revenue gap review and escalation

*PT Nusantara Seluler (fictional). Owner: Head of Sales Planning & Performance. Version 2026.1.*

## Purpose
Make sure every territory that is behind plan has a named owner, a diagnosed cause and an approved action within one working day.

## Definitions
- **Raw attainment** = actual revenue / target revenue (month-to-date, pro-rata).
- **Expected revenue** = revenue the territory should deliver given its market potential (population, purchasing power, coverage), before non-controllable events.
- **Adjusted attainment** = (actual revenue + estimated loss from non-controllable events) / expected revenue.
- **Non-controllable events**: network outages, natural disasters, competitor price actions. Logged in the shock event register by Network Operations or the Branch Marketing Officer.

## Severity
| Tier | Trigger | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Forecast month-end attainment < 90% **and** a sudden drop (> 30% day-on-day in a desa or kecamatan), or a confirmed event | Immediate message to the responsible owner |
| P2 | MTD attainment > 10 points below pro-rata | Morning digest |
| P3 | Small or slowly developing gaps | Weekly summary |

## Steps
1. **Detect.** The morning job (07:00 WIB) computes MTD attainment and a month-end forecast per cluster and kecamatan.
2. **Route.** Each alert goes only to the role that can act: Sales Supervisor (kecamatan), Cluster Sales Manager (cluster), Branch Manager (branch). Regional General Managers receive the provincial digest and unresolved escalations only.
3. **Clarify.** If the drop may be caused by the network, the owner is asked "Is this caused by a site outage?" before any action is proposed. Network-caused gaps are routed to the Regional Network Operations Manager and excluded from the sales team's controllable gap.
4. **Act.** The owner proposes or approves an action plan (outlet activation, route change, stock replenishment, coaching, backfill request). Plans are approved by the next-level manager.
5. **Escalate.** An unacknowledged P1 escalates once to the next level after 4 working hours, and once more to the Regional General Manager after a further 8 hours. No repeated reminders.
6. **Close.** The alert is resolved when the metric returns to within 5 points of pro-rata, or the gap is reclassified as non-controllable.

## Rules
- Maximum 3 interrupting messages per person per day. No messages during quiet hours (21:00-07:00 local time, Friday 11:30-13:00).
- "Not my territory" responses must be reviewed by Sales Planning within 2 working days to correct ownership data.
