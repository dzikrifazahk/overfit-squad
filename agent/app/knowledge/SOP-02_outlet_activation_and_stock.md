# SOP-02 Outlet activation, NusaPOS adoption and recharge stock

*PT Nusantara Seluler (fictional). Owner: Head of Channel Management. Version 2026.1.*

## Purpose
Keep at least 80% of registered outlets active every month and recharge stock available at 97% of outlet-days or more.

## Responsibilities
- **Channel Officer (cluster):** activation and re-activation of outlets, NusaPOS onboarding, stock monitoring.
- **Sales Supervisor (kecamatan):** plans partner canvasser routes so every outlet is visited per its call cycle.
- **Distributor partner:** employs canvassers and keeps voucher/recharge stock; bound by the partner agreement.
- **Branch Channel Manager:** reviews partner performance monthly.

## Activation playbook (when active outlets fall below 75%)
1. Pull the list of outlets inactive for 30+ days, ranked by last 3-month recharge value.
2. Prioritise the top 10-15 outlets per canvasser per day; plan routes by kecamatan.
3. Check stock: if recharge availability < 95%, raise a replenishment request with the distributor the same day.
4. Pair new canvassers (tenure < 3 months) with an experienced canvasser for the first two weeks.
5. Re-check activation after 7 days; escalate to the Branch Channel Manager if no improvement.

## Call cycle
| Outlet type | Visit frequency |
|---|---|
| konter_pulsa | weekly |
| minimarket | weekly |
| warung | every 2 weeks |
| agen | every 2 weeks |

## Effective call
A visit is an *effective call* when the outlet places an order or completes a NusaPOS transaction during the visit. Target effective-call rate: 70%.
