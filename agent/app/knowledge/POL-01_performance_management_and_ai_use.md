# POL-01 Performance management and use of AI in people processes

*PT Nusantara Seluler (fictional). Owner: Head of People Partner (Sales). Version 2026.1. Applies together with the PKB (collective labour agreement).*

## Performance cycle
- Scorecards are reviewed monthly and rated at year end. Results ("what", KPI scorecard) are combined with behaviour ("how", AKHLAK values: Amanah, Kompeten, Harmonis, Loyal, Adaptif, Kolaboratif).
- KPI achievement is capped between 0% and 150% before weighting.
- Comparisons between people are only made within cohorts of similar territories (java_urban, java_rural, outside_java_urban, outside_java_rural, 3t) and using adjusted attainment.

## Consequence management
- Missing targets is a coaching matter first. Formal warnings follow the sequence SP1 -> SP2 -> SP3 and are decided by the line manager with HR, never by a system.
- No termination can be based on target achievement alone; it requires a breach of company regulations or the PKB after the warning sequence.

## Use of AI and people analytics
1. AI may draft coaching notes, development plans, recognition messages and evidence summaries. A manager must review and edit drafts before they are saved or sent.
2. AI must not produce ratings, rankings of individuals, bonus decisions, warning letters or termination recommendations.
3. Team-level people metrics (engagement, attrition, tenure mix) are only shown for groups of 5 or more people; sensitive metrics (engagement) need 5 or more respondents.
4. Individual flight-risk scores are not shown to line managers.
5. Partner (distributor) sales staff are not employees and are not individually assessed; only aggregate capacity and activity are used.
6. Sensitive attributes (health, religion, union membership, ethnicity) are never used.
7. Every AI recommendation that leads to an action is recorded in the audit log with the approving manager.
8. Employees can see the explanation of their own scorecard and adjusted attainment.
