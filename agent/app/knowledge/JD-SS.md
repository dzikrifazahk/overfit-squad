# Sales Supervisor

Sales Supervisor (cluster level, grade BP-V). Responsible for revenue and subscriber growth in assigned kecamatan.
Key responsibilities:
- Plan and supervise partner canvasser routes in assigned kecamatan
- Drive outlet activation and recharge sell-through
- Track net adds and churn; report issues to the Cluster Sales Manager
KPI ownership (A=accountable, R=responsible): REV_TOTAL (R), REV_DATA (R), REV_VOICE_SMS (R), SUBS_ACTIVE (R), NET_ADD (R).
