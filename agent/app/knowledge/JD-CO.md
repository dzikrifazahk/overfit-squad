# Channel Officer

Channel Officer (cluster level, grade BP-V). Responsible for outlet activation, NusaPOS adoption and voucher/recharge stock availability in the cluster.
Key responsibilities:
- Activate and re-activate outlets
- Monitor recharge stock and resolve stock-outs with the distributor
- Onboard outlets to NusaPOS
KPI ownership (A=accountable, R=responsible): OUTLET_ACTIVE (R), RECHARGE_AVAIL (R).
